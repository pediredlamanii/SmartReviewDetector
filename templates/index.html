✦ Core Features

Everything you need for smarter shopping

Each feature is powered by real AI — no manual guesswork, no outdated data.

🔍

Cross-Platform Price Search

Paste any product URL and we automatically find the same product on Amazon, Flipkart, and other major e-commerce platforms — giving you a side-by-side comparison.

⚙ How it works

Our scraper extracts the product name/ID, then searches across platforms using intelligent keyword matching to surface the best matches and their live prices.

🤖

AI Fake Review Detection

Our NLP model scans every review for patterns typical of fake or paid reviews — excessive caps, repetitive phrasing, unusual sentiment spikes, and more.

⚙ How it works

Reviews are preprocessed using NLTK/spaCy, vectorized, and passed through a trained classifier (Scikit-Learn) that classifies each review as Genuine or Suspicious with a confidence score.

🏆

Trust Score & Recommendations

Every product gets a Trust Score (0–100%) that accounts for the percentage of fake reviews detected — so you see the real rating, not the inflated one.

⚙ How it works

Trust Score = Adjusted rating factoring in fake review percentage, total reviews, and cross-platform consistency. We then recommend the best platform for price and trust combined.

📊

Multi-Platform Comparison Table

See a clean, sortable table comparing the same product's price, star rating, review count, fake review %, and adjusted rating across all found platforms in one view.

⚙ How it works

After scraping each platform, results are normalized and aggregated into a unified comparison matrix. Color-coded indicators show which stats are good or concerning at a glance.

✍️

Single Review Text Analyzer

Have a specific review you're suspicious about? Paste any raw review text into our analyzer and get an instant verdict — Genuine or Fake — with reasoning and confidence.

⚙ How it works

The same NLP pipeline used for bulk analysis runs on your single review, explaining which linguistic signals (e.g. excessive exclamations, generic praise) triggered the verdict.

🔒

Secure User Accounts

Create a free account to save your analysis history and get personalized product reports. Your data is stored securely and never shared with third parties.

⚙ How it works

Authentication is handled via Flask sessions with password hashing. Your account keeps track of every product you've analyzed so you can revisit results anytime.
