About SmartReviewDetector

SmartReviewDetector is an AI-powered web application designed to help consumers make informed purchasing decisions across various e-commerce platforms. It uses real Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing to separate genuine reviews from paid or fake ones — and to tell you exactly where to buy for the best price and highest trust.

🔬 How It Works

  • Cross-Platform Search: Automatically searches for the same product across Amazon, Flipkart, and more, extracting live price and rating data.
  • Review Analysis: Scrapes customer reviews and uses Natural Language Processing (NLTK / spaCy) to clean, tokenize, and analyze the text.
  • Fake Review Detection: A trained Scikit-Learn classifier identifies patterns common in fake reviews — excessive enthusiasm, repetitive keywords, generic language, and more.
  • Trust Score Calculation: A composite Trust Score is computed from the fake-review percentage and authentic review ratings, giving you an adjusted, honest product rating.
  • Smart Recommendation: The system recommends the best platform based on a combination of price, trust score, and review authenticity.

🛠 Technology Stack

Python 3 Flask Scikit-Learn NLTK spaCy BeautifulSoup Requests SQLite HTML / CSS / JS

🎯 Why It Matters

Fake reviews are a massive problem — studies show up to 30–40% of online reviews may be inauthentic. They inflate product ratings, mislead buyers, and distort market competition. SmartReviewDetector fights back with transparent, explainable AI so you always know what you're actually buying.
